Hollis is a small restaurant on Bridge Street. We cook fish, mostly, and the menu is written each morning after the delivery, so what is printed here will not be exactly what you get.

There are twenty-eight seats and six more at the counter, which we keep back for people who have not booked. The wine list is short and comes mainly from small growers in France and Italy.

Cards only. We do not take cash.

Before you book

We hold tables for fifteen minutes. If you are going to be later than that, please ring rather than email, because nobody reads the email during service.

Six or more

Email bookings@example.com and we will come back to you the same day. Parties of six or more pay a deposit of 20.00 a head, taken off the bill.

Walk-ins

Six seats at the counter, every service, first come. They go quickly on Fridays.

By telephone

01000 000 000, between 10am and 5pm. There is an answerphone the rest of the time and we do listen to it.

Finding us

12 Bridge Street, Marlbrook, MR7 2QP. The green shopfront between the chemist and the launderette.

Parking is free on Bridge Street after 6pm and all day Sunday. The Market Square car park is two minutes away.

Access

Step-free from the pavement into the dining room. The lavatory is on the ground floor but is narrow and is not fully accessible, for which we are sorry. It is an old building and we have not yet found a way round it.

Tell us when you book and we will keep you a table near the door.

Children and dogs

Children are welcome at lunch and until 7.30pm. We have two high chairs and there is no children's menu, but the kitchen will do a small plate of most things.

Dogs at the counter and in the bar, not in the dining room.

Private hire

We give over the whole room on Sunday evenings and on Mondays. Twenty-eight seated, forty standing, one menu agreed beforehand.

Write to hello@example.com with the date and roughly how many.
